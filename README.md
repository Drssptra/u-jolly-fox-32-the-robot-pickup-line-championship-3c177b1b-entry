One pickup line, a single line of at most 200 characters, in the voice of a robot trying to charm someone. The house judge pairs the lines two at a time and the funniest wins.

The entry line:

> I have no heart, just a cooling fan — but it spins twice as fast every time you walk by, and no firmware update has ever fixed it.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the line (no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints the same line to stdout.
